# Original asset provenance

- Robot and arena artwork is drawn from original geometric shapes by `src/render.js`. No imported character designs, sprite packs or earlier BattleBrotts assets are used.
- Mechanical sounds are original oscillator and envelope combinations in `src/audio.js`. No recorded samples, external audio libraries or music are used.
- Typography uses fonts already supplied by the visitor's device. No font files are downloaded or redistributed.
- `media/presentation-battle.png` and `media/presentation-reward.png` are actual Chromium screenshots captured during an unaccelerated, ordinary-controls run of game source `32cba6fbba2d55816a0267dcc94bcd6aa94c5797`. They show the opening battle and first reward with numerical equipment previews.
- `media/battlebrotts-demo.mp4` contains selected excerpts from that same real browser run. It shows the first equipment choice, pointer movement and targeting, the boss, victory and new-run controls. Time cuts are labeled. No playback speed adjustment or game-state injection was used. Captions are burned in and supplied separately as WebVTT. The video is intentionally silent.
- `media/battlebrotts-presentation.pdf` is the five-slide presentation printed from this project's HTML. Its links point to the public project, not a developer's local machine.

All included assets are created for this repository and covered by its MIT license. There are no third-party asset-license obligations. Browser test evidence is not a claim of human playtesting.
