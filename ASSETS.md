# Original asset provenance

- Robot and arena artwork is drawn from original geometric shapes by `src/render.js`. No imported character designs, sprite packs or earlier studio assets are used.
- Mechanical sounds are original oscillator and envelope combinations in `src/audio.js`. No recorded samples, external audio libraries or music are used.
- Typography uses fonts already supplied by the visitor's device. No font files are downloaded or redistributed.
- `media/linked-pair.png`, `media/link-broken.png` and `media/coil-pulse.png` are unchanged pixel crops from an ordinary-controls Chromium run of expansion game source [`c849d4b`](https://github.com/brotatotes/battlebrotts-reborn/tree/c849d4b8c743f8d8b265e6d179b39a11ba5dde21). `media/barrel-pierce.png` is from a separate Long Barrel run of that source. Only the Coil run uses a normal target click to focus Relay.
- `media/classic-shot.png` is an unchanged pixel crop from a fresh recording of the original game source [`8fadd89`](https://github.com/brotatotes/battlebrotts-reborn/tree/8fadd89528e3de5100b0050c61f5030f10cacf5b). No recreated gameplay, synthetic effects or added impact artwork appears in these screenshots.
- `media/battlebrotts-demo.mp4` is a new 63.96-second edit of these three real recordings. Six excerpts show Classic, the Coil reward choice, Relay's defeat, the later Coil cluster encounter, a separate Barrel piercing encounter, and the Coil run's Chief win and new-run control. Separate runs and time cuts are labeled. No playback speed adjustment or game-state injection was used. Captions are burned in and supplied separately as WebVTT. The video is intentionally silent. SHA256 is `b88ac48acaf6990a2a2021970e1ca8c6ff906e0ca4f60314706999c5fbf32eea`.
- `media/battlebrotts-presentation.pdf` is the refreshed five-slide presentation printed from this project's HTML. Its links point to the public project, not a developer's local machine.
- The original version 1.0.0 release, video and presentation remain unchanged in the [original release](https://github.com/brotatotes/battlebrotts-reborn/releases/tag/v1.0.0). The `classic/` route preserves its gameplay bytes, with small explanatory media pages instead of another copy of the old large video.
